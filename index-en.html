About me

Hello! My name is Olga, and I am a Catalan and Spanish teacher with over 8 years of experience teaching both languages. I am passionate about sharing my linguistic and cultural knowledge with my students, helping them to use both languages with confidence.

In my classes, I use interactive methods tailored to each student's individual needs. I believe in the importance of total immersion and constant practice, which is why my lessons are dynamic, focused on real communication and supported by authentic materials. These materials help students get to know Catalan and Spanish culture first-hand. I am here to help you achieve your linguistic goals and enjoy the learning process. It's a pleasure to meet you, so why wait to improve your Catalan and Spanish?

How it works

My main goal is for my students to speak Spanish and Catalan fluently, so I prioritise conversation in my classes. Throughout our lessons, we'll engage in dynamic, lively discussions while I correct any mistakes. This process helps establish a strong language foundation through repetition. At the same time, we'll gradually incorporate grammar and vocabulary to enhance overall language proficiency.

Extra homework

I don't assign homework, as I understand that each student has their own schedule, priorities, and responsibilities. However, if you'd like some homework, just let me know and I'll be happy to set it.

Weekly progress

If you'd like to practise writing, I also offer the option of correcting one essay per week on any topic. This gives you the chance to practise writing and track your progress over time.

Free first class

For the first few lessons, I'll provide all the necessary materials. If you have any other questions, feel free to ask during your free 30-minute trial class or contact me directly.
